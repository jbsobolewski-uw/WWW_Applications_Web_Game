How to Play Hex Sweeper

Forget what you know about corners. This is classic mine-clearing logic, reimagined on a hexagonal grid.

Core Mechanics

  • The Goal: Reveal every safe tile on the board without detonating a single mine.
  • The Numbers: A revealed number tells you exactly how many mines are hidden in the 6 directly adjacent hexagons.
  • Chain Reactions: If you click a tile with zero adjacent mines, the board will automatically reveal all neighboring safe tiles.

Controls

  • Reveal Tile: Left-click (or tap on mobile).
  • Place Flag: Right-click (or long-press on mobile) to mark a suspected mine. This locks the tile so you don't accidentally click it.
💡 Pro Tip: Don't sweat your first move! Your very first click is procedurally guaranteed to be safe. Speed matters, so clear the board as fast as you can to climb the global leaderboard!
